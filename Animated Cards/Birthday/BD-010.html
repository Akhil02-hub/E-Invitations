spoil the little one

A Few Little Wishes

Please don't go overboard — your presence truly is the greatest present. But if you'd like to spoil Baby Wren, here are three gentle hints.

A Library of Love

In lieu of a card, bring a favorite book to start Wren's very first shelf — classics, new finds, or a story you loved as a child.

The Diaper Drop

Sizes 1 & 2, wipes, lotions and burp cloths — the practical magic every new nest quietly needs.

Thoughtful & Easy

A gift card to her favorite baby shop or bookstore is always a perfect fit — for any size and any season.

"In place of a card, a book is welcome here —
a story you loved, for Wren's first year.
Sign the cover with a note or your name,
a keepsake of love that will always remain."
