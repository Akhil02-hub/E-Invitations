a message written in stardust

Something has been
waiting for you

Turn your sound on, take a breath,
then press begin — and watch what the sky has been keeping.

I hid a thousand feelings among these stars…

…and every single one of them is yours.

so here it is —

Will you be mine, forever?

the stars have already decided.

and the whole sky lit up —

Forever, then.

I love you, my love — today, tomorrow, and every star after.

— from the one who loves you most
