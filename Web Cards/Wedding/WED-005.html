How it began

A quiet café,
a rainy Tuesday,
one shared umbrella.

Seven years ago, Sebastian offered his umbrella to a stranger rushing past a bookshop window. That stranger became his favourite hello, his travel companion, his calm in every storm — and now, his wife.

“Whatever our souls are made of, his and mine are the same.”
— Emily Brontë

We would be honoured to have you beside us as we begin this next chapter — surrounded by wildflowers, candlelight, and the warmth of everyone who made us who we are.

With love, Amelia & Sebastian
