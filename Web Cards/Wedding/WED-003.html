Our story

A little luck,
a lot of laughter

From a rainy evening in Lisbon to a sunlit kitchen in Florence — here are the chapters that brought us to this day.

Autumn 2018

The first conversation

A shared umbrella outside a tiny bookshop in Lisbon, and a conversation that lasted until the café closed.

Summer 2021

A home with olive trees

Two suitcases, one stubborn cat, and a farmhouse kitchen where every Sunday became a celebration.

Spring 2025

The question, at golden hour

On a hillside above Val d'Orcia, with the light going pink and both of us crying before the words came out.

September 2026

The wedding day

And now, the best part — having the people we love around one long table, under the Tuscan sky.
