Our Story

A quiet kind of forever

Since2018
01 — The Beginning

A rainy evening,
a shared umbrella

We met on a grey October evening outside a tiny bookshop in Milan, both waiting for the rain to stop. Matteo offered an umbrella; Elena offered coffee in return.

Four hours later, the rain had long stopped and neither of us had noticed. Some conversations simply never end — they just keep becoming a life.

02 — The Question

A proposal beneath
the olive trees

Eight summers later, on a hillside above Lake Como at the exact hour the light turns gold, Matteo asked. Elena said yes before he finished the sentence.

Now we would like nothing more than to begin this next chapter surrounded by the family and friends who carried us here — including you.
