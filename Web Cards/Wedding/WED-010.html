Our Journey

Our Story

2019 — The First Hello

A Chance Encounter

It began on a rainy afternoon at a small bookshop in San Francisco. Amara reached for the last copy of a poetry collection at the exact moment Julian did. Coffee followed the argument over who saw it first — and neither of them wanted the conversation to end.

2021 — Falling in Love

Two Years of Adventures

From sunrise hikes in Yosemite to getting wonderfully lost in the streets of Kyoto, every adventure only drew them closer. They built a love rooted in laughter, quiet Sunday mornings, and the certainty that home was wherever they were together.

2025 — The Proposal

A Sunset Promise

On a golden evening overlooking the Napa Valley vineyards, Julian knelt with a ring and a heart full of hope. Through happy tears, Amara said yes before he could even finish the question. Now, they invite you to witness the beginning of forever.
