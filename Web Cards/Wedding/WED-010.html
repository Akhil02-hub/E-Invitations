The Craft

Made slowly, felt instantly.

Each suite passes through seven pairs of hands — from the first typographic sketch to the final wax seal. We print on century-old Heidelberg presses, then finish every edge by hand.

Hand-drawn typography

Bespoke monograms and calligraphy drawn for your names — never a template.

Dimensional layering

Vellum wraps, floating panels and ribbon pulls that create real depth in hand.

On-time, every time

A dedicated producer, weekly proof updates and tracked delivery to your door.

How It Unfolds

A considered four-step journey

Clear milestones, generous revisions and a producer beside you from the first sketch to the sealed envelope.

01

Discovery

A relaxed consultation about your story, palette, venue and the feeling you want guests to have.

02

Design

Two distinct directions, refined with you over two generous rounds of revisions.

03

Production

Printing, foiling, edge painting and hand assembly — with progress photos along the way.

04

Delivery

Boxed, tissue-wrapped and shipped worldwide, plus digital RSVP setup if you wish.

Materials

Paper you can hear unfold.

We source cotton rag papers from three family-run mills, print with vegetable-based inks, and finish with genuine wax — because the tactile moment is half the memory.

Responsibly sourced

FSC-certified cotton rag, tree-free fibres and carbon-balanced shipping on every order.

Archival inks & foils

Colour-fast for generations — your invitation becomes an heirloom, not a throwaway.
