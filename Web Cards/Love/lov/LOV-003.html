My promises to you

What I will strive to be

Not perfect — only Allah is perfect — but always trying, always sincere.

  • 01

    A companion in faith

    To pray beside you, remind you of Allah when you forget, and let you remind me when I do. To walk together toward Jannah.

  • 02

    Respect & gentleness

    Following the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, who said "The best of you are those who are best to their wives." I will honour you in words and actions.

  • 03

    Honour for your family

    Your parents will be my parents. I will come to them the right way — with respect, honesty, and their blessing.

  • 04

    Support for your dreams

    Your goals, your studies, your passions — I will be your biggest supporter, never a barrier.

  • 05

    Patience in every season

    In ease and in hardship, with sabr and shukr, I will stand by you and keep making du'a for us.
