Order Of The Day

A beautifully unhurried afternoon

Come for the whole celebration or drop in for your favourite part — there's something lovely happening at every hour.

2:00 PM

Arrivals & Garden Cocktails

Sparkling elderflower, fresh lemonade and a live acoustic set on the terrace while everyone settles in.

2:45 PM

Welcome Toast

A few words from the parents-to-be, plus a heartfelt thank you to everyone who travelled to be here.

3:15 PM

Games, Blooms & Wishes

Plant-a-wish station, baby bingo, and a keepsake book where every guest leaves a note for Ellis.

4:15 PM

Afternoon Tea & Cake

A tiered spread of scones, seasonal tarts and the lemon-elderflower cake everyone's been waiting for.

5:15 PM

Gifts & Goodbyes

Slow unwrapping, warm hugs, and a little take-home treat as the golden hour settles over the garden.
