The beginning

Celebrating a love
that's already here

After months of quiet dreaming, tiny kicks and a whole lot of nursery planning, we're finally ready to celebrate with the people who made this journey so special. Come for the cake, stay for the belly laughs.

  • An intimate garden afternoon with 40 of our favourite people — and one very small guest of honour.
  • Brunch-style grazing table, floral lemonade bar and a dessert spread worth saving room for.
  • Games that are actually fun, a wishes-for-baby keepsake book, and plenty of time to just be together.
Hosted with love by Maya & Daniel and the wonderful Auntie Rose
