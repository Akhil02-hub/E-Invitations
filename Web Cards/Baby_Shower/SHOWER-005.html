Our Story

Four years of waiting,
one very loved little soul

From a first coffee date in the rain to assembling a crib at midnight, this chapter has been written slowly and joyfully. Now, with the nursery painted and the tiny socks finally folded, we'd love nothing more than to celebrate surrounded by the people who carried us here.

Your presence is the only gift we need — though if you'd like to bring something, a handwritten note for baby's memory book would mean the world.

Order of the Day

An afternoon, beautifully unhurried

Here's how the celebration will unfold. Nothing is rigid — stay for the parts you love, linger where you like.

2:00 PM

Arrivals & Welcome Sips

Rosemary lemonade, sparkling elderflower and a first wander through the garden terrace.

2:30 PM

Garden Brunch is Served

A seasonal grazing table with vegetarian mains, wood-fired flatbreads and a very serious dessert cart.

3:15 PM

Games & Well-Wishes

Guess the due date, decorate a onesie, and leave a message for baby to read on their eighteenth birthday.

4:00 PM

Gifts & Cake

Vanilla and elderflower cake cut at four, followed by a slow, laughter-filled gift opening on the lawn.

5:00 PM

Farewell Favors

Head home with a small jar of local honey and our endless gratitude for being part of this chapter.

Mood & Moments

A glimpse of the day to come

Soft linens, wild garden florals, hand-lettered signage and tables dressed in the tones of early summer.

Yes — complimentary valet is available at the west garden gate from 1:45 PM, along with a free self-park lot on NW Overton Street. The venue is also a five-minute walk from the Streetcar Loop.
