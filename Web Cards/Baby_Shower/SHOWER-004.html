Our Story

Four years, two cities,
one very small heartbeat.

Good to Know

A few little things

Can I bring children?

Absolutely. There's a supervised lawn corner with games, cushions and a dedicated snack table for smaller guests.

What if it rains?

The Glasshouse is fully covered and heated — the celebration goes ahead beautifully whatever the sky decides.

Where should I park?

Free parking is available on the north lawn entrance, with overflow on Willow Lane just a two-minute walk away.

Is there a hashtag?

Yes — #HelloBabyMiller. Tag your photos so we can collect every moment in one place.

Dietary requirements?

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are on every table. Just mention anything specific in your RSVP.

Who can I ask?

Reach our sister and organiser, Sophie, at hello@babymiller.com — she answers fast.
