Why we're gathering

An evening composed like a piece of music.

From the moment the sun dips behind the vineyard, the night unfolds in movements — a welcome pour, a long-table dinner under strung lights, speeches that make everyone laugh and cry, and a dance floor that refuses to empty.

Bring your stories, your appetite, and your best pair of dancing shoes. Everything else — the flowers, the firelight, the fireworks — is already arranged.
