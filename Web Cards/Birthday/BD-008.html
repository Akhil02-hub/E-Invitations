The story behind the evening

Three decades, one golden gathering.

Some milestones deserve more than a message. They deserve a room full of the people who witnessed the journey — grandparents, cousins, childhood friends and the family that grew along the way.

Expect long tables dressed in ivory and brass, a menu built around family recipes, a live string ensemble at dusk, and a toast that will probably end with happy tears. Bring your stories. We'll bring the cake.
